# hardware/stl/

Generated by [`../generate.py`](../generate.py) and committed, so you can print
without installing anything. **Don't edit these by hand** — they are output,
and the next run overwrites them.

Open a plate in your slicer and everything arrives laid out and correctly
oriented. **Don't rotate anything.**

| Plate | Filament | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| `plate1_black.3mf` | Black | `body`, `back`, `beak` |
| `plate2_white.3mf` | White | `belly`, `chassis`, `eyes` |

The individual `.stl` files are the same six parts, unpacked, for when you want
to arrange them yourself.

0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, **no supports on any part**. Full settings,
filament weights and the face options:
[docs/hardware/printing.md](../../docs/hardware/printing.md).

Then build it: [docs/hardware/assembly.md](../../docs/hardware/assembly.md).
